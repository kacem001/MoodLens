Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

MoodLens (“we”, “our”) is a free web tool created by Kacem El Amine under KacemLabs. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use this website.

1. Camera and images

MoodLens analyzes camera frames and images you choose entirely inside your browser. The AI models are downloaded to your device and run locally with TensorFlow.js. Your video, photos and the resulting emotion scores are not uploaded, stored or shared with us or any third party.

Camera access is requested only after you press “Start camera” and stops when you press “Stop camera”, switch tabs inside the app or close the page. You can revoke permission at any time in your browser settings.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account and do not collect names, emails, biometric templates or face data. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. The site does not currently use analytics; if that changes, this policy will be updated.

3. Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages to deliver and secure the service. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

4. Third-party links

The donation button links to Ko-fi and our contact page links to Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, GitHub and a personal portfolio. These services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their practices.

5. Local browser storage

MoodLens does not set cookies. Your browser may cache the AI model files to load the site faster; this cache stays on your device.

6. Children’s privacy

MoodLens is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because nothing is uploaded, no child’s image is ever received by us.

7. Accuracy and permitted use

Emotion results are statistical estimates of visible facial expressions and may be wrong. MoodLens is provided for educational and entertainment purposes and must not be used for medical, psychological, employment, security, legal or other decisions about people. Only analyze images of people who have agreed to it.

8. Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have any questions about privacy, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision.
