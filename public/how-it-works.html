Under the hood

How MoodLens works

From a camera frame to an emotion score in five steps, all on your device in a fraction of a second.

Step 1

Capture

Your camera frame or uploaded photo is read locally.

Step 2

Detect face

A neural detector finds the largest face and its position.

Step 3

Align and crop

68 facial landmarks frame the face from brows to chin.

Step 4

Prepare input

The crop becomes a 48×48 grayscale image, contrast-balanced.

Step 5

Classify

A CNN outputs a probability for each of the 7 emotions.

The seven emotions

Categories come from the FER2013 facial expression dataset.

Angry

Furrowed, lowered brows, tense eyes and pressed lips.

Disgust

Wrinkled nose, raised upper lip and narrowed eyes.

Fear

Raised brows, wide eyes and a tense, open mouth.

Happy

Raised cheeks, crinkled eyes and upturned lip corners.

Sad

Drooping lip corners, lowered gaze and inner brows lifted.

Surprise

Arched brows, widened eyes and a dropped jaw.

Neutral

A relaxed face with no strong muscle movement.

Tips for accurate results

  • Use even, front-facing light; avoid harsh shadows.
  • Look toward the camera with your whole face visible.
  • Remove sunglasses, masks or hands covering the face.
  • Keep a natural distance, about an arm’s length.
  • Use a sharp, high-resolution photo for uploads.

Limitations

  • It detects facial expressions, not true feelings.
  • Accuracy varies with lighting, pose, age and image quality.
  • Subtle, mixed or culturally specific expressions are hard to classify.
  • Not suitable for medical, hiring, security or legal decisions.

Technology

  • TensorFlow.js runs the neural networks in your browser with WebGL acceleration.
  • SSD MobileNet detects faces and a 68-point landmark model frames them from brows to chin.
  • A custom convolutional neural network trained on FER2013 classifies the 48×48 crop.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo or video uploaded anywhere?

No. Face detection and emotion classification run in your browser using TensorFlow.js. Camera frames and uploaded images are never sent to a server.

Why do I get different results in different lighting?

The model reads pixel patterns from a small grayscale crop, so shadows, strong backlight and low resolution can change the result. Face a light source and look at the camera.

Does MoodLens know how I really feel?

No. It estimates which of seven expression categories your visible facial expression most resembles. Real emotions are far more complex than a facial pose.

Why is the camera not starting?

Allow camera permission in your browser. Cameras only work on secure pages (HTTPS or localhost). Make sure no other app is using the camera.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, including Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. A device with WebGL support gives the smoothest live results.

Is MoodLens free?

Yes. It is free to use. If you enjoy it, you can support the project with a donation.
